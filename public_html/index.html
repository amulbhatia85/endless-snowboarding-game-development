The descent

  • Real mountains, not waves

    Deep, sustained descents carved from the terrain itself — long falls, rounded valley floors, and climbs that stop just short of where you started.

  • Catch the crest

    Ride a sharp crest fast enough and the mountain lets you go. No button and no ramp — just momentum, a tangent to follow, and whatever air your speed can buy.

  • Chase the glowstones

    Chain them along your line, build the combo, and bank the run. A clean descent always pays better than a fast, messy one.

  • One long ride

    Levels string together into a journey across the range — green hills, snowfall, mist, a moonlit forest, jungle canopy — each with its own target and weather.

On the mountain

No account. No signal needed.

The whole game runs offline. Scores, achievements and preferences stay in local storage on your own device — nothing is asked of you, and nothing leaves the mountain.
