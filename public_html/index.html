The descent

Gravity is the whole game.

Point it downhill and let the mountain do the work. Nothing is scripted — every slope is generated, and every valley bottom is somewhere to earn back the height you just spent. Carry your speed out of a valley and you decide how high the next mountain takes you.

01

Real descents

Deep, sustained runs carved from the terrain itself — not waves of flat ground. Long falls, rounded valley floors, and climbs that fall just short of where you started.

02

Crest launches

Ride a sharp crest fast enough and the mountain lets you go. No button, no ramp — just momentum, a tangent to follow, and as much air as your speed can buy.

03

Glowstones

Collect them along the line, chain your combos, and bank the run. A clean descent pays far better than a fast, messy one.

04

A journey, not a loop

Levels string together into a run across the range, each with its own target and its own weather. Fall and you can take the mountain up on its offer to revive.

Five biomes

Every ridge has its own light.

The range changes under you as you ride — from green foothills to snowfall, to mist, to a forest lit only by the moon, and out into jungle canopy.

Yours, quietly

No account. No server. No signal needed.

Afterglow is built to be yours alone. Everything you earn stays on the device in your hand — no sign-up, no cloud, nothing to lose when your signal does.

  • Play it on a plane

    The whole game runs offline. No download on first launch, no connection checks, no waiting.

  • Progress kept on your device

    Scores, achievements and preferences live in local storage. No account ever asked for.

  • Nothing following you home

    No analytics on this page, and no tracking in the game beyond the ads that keep it free.
